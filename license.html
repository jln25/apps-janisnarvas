3 · One clarification

The BAMS curriculum

The BAMS Planner lists the course codes, titles and unit counts of the UPOU Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies program. That curriculum belongs to the UPOU, not mine — it is reproduced here as factual reference so students can track their own progress, and nothing above claims any rights over it. The course ordering in the planner follows the BAMS curriculum flowchart by Miguel Ferdinand C. Co, used with thanks; that work is his and is not covered by the licences above. It is also unofficial and a work-in-progress; check anything that matters against UPOU's own published curriculum before you rely on it for enrollment decisions.
