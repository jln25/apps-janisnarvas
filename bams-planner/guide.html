BAMS Planner User Guide

The BAMS Planner is a free tool for mapping your way through the UPOU Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies. It keeps count of your units, lays your courses out term by term, and warns you when a course is not open to you yet. Open the planner.

1 · What this is

The planner holds the full BAMS curriculum — every GE, Common, Mandated, Major, Production and Capstone course — and lets you record where you are in it. Nothing is filled in for you: you start with every course unmarked and build the plan yourself.

It is unofficial. It is a planning aid, not an enrolment system and not an academic record. Confirm anything that matters with your program adviser or UPOU's own published curriculum before you act on it.

2 · Where your data lives

There is no account and no login. Everything you enter is stored in your own browser on the device you are using, and never leaves it.

That has one consequence worth understanding: clearing your browsing data will erase your plan, and your plan will not follow you to another device or browser. If you have spent time on it, export a copy (see section 8).

3 · The progress dashboard

The panel at the top counts only courses you have marked Completed. It shows your total against the 142 units the degree requires, then a card per category so you can see which part of the curriculum is lagging.

PE and NSTP sit in their own row underneath. They are required, but they do not count toward the 142 units, so they are tracked separately rather than folded into the main total.

4 · The course list

Every course starts under No term assigned. Think of that as the pool you plan out of — as you give courses a term, they move up into their own group, and the list reads top-down in the order you intend to take them.

The two dropdowns above the list narrow what you are looking at, by category or by status. They only filter the view; nothing is changed or lost while a filter is on.

5 · Marking a course

Each course takes one of four states:

  • — untouched; not yet part of your plan
  • Planned you intend to take it in the term you choose
  • In progress you are taking it now
  • Completed you have passed it — this is what the dashboard counts

Elective groups have a ceiling. The GE electives count 9 units toward the degree and the major electives 15, so the planner will stop you marking more than the curriculum can credit.

6 · Planning by term

Terms are picked from a list rather than typed, so the planner can tell which term comes first. Once you have assigned any, a Plan by term panel appears above the list showing each term's unit load — the quickest way to catch a term you have overloaded.

7 · Prerequisites

Courses that need something first say so under their title. A course can only be marked Planned once each prerequisite is either already passed, or planned in an earlier term — you cannot take a course and its prerequisite in the same term.

Marking a course Completed or In progress is never blocked. You may have credit, a substitution or consent of instructor that the planner cannot know about, so recording what actually happened always takes priority over the rule.

Some entries show Suggested after instead. Those come from the curriculum flowchart's recommended sequence rather than a formal requirement, so they advise without blocking anything.

8 · Exporting your plan

Export CSV downloads every course with its status and term, ready to open in a spreadsheet. Export PDF opens your browser's print dialogue — choose Save as PDF as the destination to keep a copy of your Plan of Study. The printed version always comes out on white, whatever theme you are using.

9 · Where the curriculum comes from

Course codes, titles and units follow UPOU's published BAMS curriculum. Prerequisites come from the individual FICS course pages, and the recommended ordering from the BAMS curriculum flowchart. Where the two disagree, the formal prerequisite is the one enforced and the flowchart's ordering is shown as a suggestion.

Curricula change. If you spot something out of date, please send a correction.

10 · A typical first session

  1. Mark everything you have already passed as Completed. The dashboard fills in as you go.
  2. Mark what you are taking now as In progress.
  3. Give your next term a few Planned courses, checking the prerequisite lines as you choose.
  4. Glance at Plan by term to see whether the load is reasonable.
  5. Export a PDF so you have a copy outside the browser.
